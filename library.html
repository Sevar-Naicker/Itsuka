On your phone

Add Itsuka to your home screen and it opens from its own icon like an app.

Adding from other apps

Android: add Itsuka to your home screen, then in Instagram, Pinterest or any app press Share and choose Itsuka. A new catalogue card opens with the link filled in.

iPhone: add the Itsuka shortcut once, then in Instagram, Pinterest or any app press Share, then More, and choose Itsuka.
