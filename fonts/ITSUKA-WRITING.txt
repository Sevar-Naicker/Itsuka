Itsuka Writing (itsuka-writing.woff2)

Itsuka Writing is the Mywriting font by Peax Webdesign, redrawn on a pixel grid so its letters are made of square dots like the rest of Itsuka.
Mywriting is listed as 100% Free by its author on DaFont: https://www.dafont.com/mywriting.font
The pixel version has its own name so it is not mistaken for the original.
