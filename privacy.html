Privacy

Last updated 20 October 2026

Itsuka is a small, free library for saving the artists, creators and shops you want to come back to someday. This page explains, in plain words, what it keeps about you, who can see it and how to delete it.

What Itsuka keeps

  • Your library card: your email address, the name you registered with, the date you joined and your card photo if you add one.
  • Your password: Itsuka never sees it. It is stored scrambled by Supabase, the service that runs the accounts, and cannot be read back by anyone.
  • Your shelves: your sections, books, links, notes and book pictures, and whether a book is marked done. Pictures are shrunk before they are stored.
  • Your settings: which shelf tools you have switched on and your colour on joint shelves, so they follow you to every device.
  • Joint shelves: which shared shelves you have joined, and who added each book on them.
  • Letters on joint shelves: a short log of what changed on each joint shelf (a book added, removed or ticked done, the shelf renamed, someone joining or leaving), with the first name of who did it, and when you last read the shelf's letter. Each change is cleared after 30 days, and your name is taken out of the log when you delete your account.
  • Your reading record: the day you ticked each book done, with its name and shelf, so it can still be listed after the book is deleted. Only you can see it. Take off removes a deleted book from it, unticking Done removes it too, and it is deleted with your account.
  • Your latest visit: the date you last opened your library, only to count how many members use Itsuka each day. It keeps no history of your visits, is replaced by each new day and is deleted with your account.

What stays on your device

Your browser keeps your sign-in and a few small choices, such as your background and whether done books are hidden. They never leave your device. Itsuka has no adverts, no tracking across other sites and no outside analytics.

So your library can open with no signal, your browser also keeps a copy of your shelves (their names, books, links and notes, not your pictures) and of the website's own files. The copy stays on your device, is brought up to date each time your shelves change, and is removed when you sign out or delete your account.

Who can see it

  • Only you can see your shelves, books and pictures, apart from the ones you choose to share or fill together.
  • A joint shelf can be seen by everyone on it: up to five people the owner invited. They see that shelf's books, links, notes and pictures, who added each book, what changed on it in the last 30 days, and each other's first names and chosen colours. Never anyone's email or other shelves. The owner can remove people and turn the invite link off, and anyone can leave. Books you add stay on the shelf when you leave or delete your account.
  • A shelf you share can be seen by anyone with its link: the shelf's name, its books, links, notes and pictures, and your first name. Never your email, your other shelves or your done marks. Stop sharing turns the link off straight away.
  • The developer can reach the database to keep the site running and fix problems, and does not look through anyone's library. To keep an eye on the free plan's limits, the developer sees totals only, such as how many members visited today or how much room the pictures take, never anyone's books, names or emails.

The services Itsuka uses

  • Supabase stores the accounts, shelves and pictures, and passes changes on a joint shelf to the people on it. Its servers may be outside South Africa.
  • Resend sends the emails Itsuka needs, such as confirming your email address and resetting a password. It is only given your email address.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website and jsDelivr delivers one of its code files. Like any website host, they see your device's internet address when a page loads.

Your information is never sold, shared for advertising or used for anything other than running your library.

Your choices

  • Take a copy: Settings, then Download a copy, saves your whole library as one file at any time.
  • Change things: edit or delete any book, section or photo whenever you like.
  • Delete your account: open your library card and press Delete my account. Your card, shelves, books, pictures and share links are deleted straight away, and this cannot be undone.

Questions

Itsuka is run by Sav and is written with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) in mind. For any question about your information, or to ask for it to be corrected or deleted, email sevarn24@gmail.com.
